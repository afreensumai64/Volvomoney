## Project Description
Finspire is a Flask finance web app that helps people understand and manage money better.
- **Budget Chachu**: animated desi AI mascot and chatbot that floats, blinks and "thinks" while the AI answers
- **News Flash**: live headlines on stocks, gold, crypto, economy and savings from trusted Indian finance sites
- **Expense Tracker**: add expenses with receipt uploads, category breakdown, monthly budget
- **Savings Planner & Guide**: goals with progress, SIP calculator, 50/30/20 rule, tips
- **Dashboard pop-ups**: personalised invest/savings suggestion cards on entry, plus rotating market news toasts
- **Chachu speaks**: mouth, head and typing animation with optional browser voice, eyes follow the cursor
- **Roles**: pre-existing Admin superuser (manages users) and regular users
